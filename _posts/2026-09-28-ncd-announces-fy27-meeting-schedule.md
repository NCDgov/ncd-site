**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

Sept. 28, 2026

**WASHINGTON--**The National Council on Disability (NCD) has announced the planned quarterly Council meetings for fiscal year 2027.

The dates are: November 9-10, 2026 in Phoenix, Arizona; February 4, 2027 (virtual); May 6-7, 2027 in Washington, D.C.; and August 5, 2027 (virtual).

All meetings will be streamed live via Zoom for Government.

NCD is an independent federal agency making recommendations to Congress, the President and federal agencies, state, local, tribal, and U.S. territory governments on disability policy.

Information on NCD meetings and events is available on our [Upcoming Events page](https://www.ncd.gov/meetings/).
